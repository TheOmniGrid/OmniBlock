# OmniBlock

A content blocker for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, and Firefox —
six protection levels, an always-on security shield, no telemetry. One
person builds it; the people who use it keep it going.

OmniBlock isn't on the Chrome Web Store or addons.mozilla.org. It's
donationware, and Ko-fi is one of the two ways to get a build — a one-time
coffee if that's what you'd rather do, no subscription required.

## Buy a coffee

Whatever you'd normally spend on this counts. There's no minimum that
means more than another — support here goes straight toward keeping filter
lists current, keeping both browser builds working, and answering the
people who report what's broken.
