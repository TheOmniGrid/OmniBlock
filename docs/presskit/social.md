## Long (forum announcement, changelog post)

> **OmniBlock — a content blocker for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera,
> and Firefox.**
>
> The pitch: blocking should be something you tune, not something you
> fight. OmniBlock ships six protection levels — from off to a
> "fortress" setting that expects some breakage — plus a Security Shield
> that blocks malware, phishing, scam, and fake-shop domains
> independently of whatever ad-blocking level you're running. Turning ads
> off should never turn phishing protection off with it; that's not a
> default you have to remember to set.
>
> Underneath: 623,896 domains blocked by default and up to 694,738 at the
> highest level, compiled from fourteen maintained filter sources. Custom
> list subscriptions, a My Filters editor with uBO-style syntax, an
> element picker, per-site trust, import/export, and a full interface in
> English, German, Spanish, French, and Romanian.
>
> No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts, no server of its own — the
> only network contact OmniBlock ever makes is to filter-list hosts and
> any custom list URL you add yourself. That's not a settings toggle;
> there's simply nothing built that could send anything anywhere else.
>
> It's honest about its limits, too. On Chromium, no Manifest V3
> extension can match MV2-era uBlock Origin's dynamic filtering — Chrome
> removed the API that made it possible, for every extension, not just
> this one. OmniBlock's real competitors on Chromium are uBO Lite and
> ABP, and the goal is to win on filter-list depth, level ergonomics, and
> per-site control, at native speed. On Firefox, full request blocking is
> still available, and OmniBlock is genuinely in uBlock Origin's own
> league.
>
> It's free software — GPL-3.0-or-later — and donationware: not on the
> Chrome Web Store, not on addons.mozilla.org. Builds go directly to
> supporters through Ko-fi (https://ko-fi.com/theomnigrid) or Patreon (https://www.patreon.com/TheOmniGrid), and if
> you can't support it right now, ask anyway — you'll get a copy.
> Installation takes about a minute; see INSTALLATION.md in the repo.
