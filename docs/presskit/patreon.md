# OmniBlock

**A cleaner web. Two browsers. No telemetry.**

## The hook

Blocking should be something you tune, not something you fight. OmniBlock
is a content blocker for Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, and Firefox —
six protection levels, an always-on security shield, and zero telemetry,
built by one person and kept alive by the people who use it.

## What OmniBlock is

OmniBlock blocks 623,896 domains out of the box and up to 694,738 at its
highest level, compiled from fourteen maintained filter sources. It's free
software (GPL-3.0-or-later) and donationware: it isn't on the Chrome Web
Store or addons.mozilla.org, and the only way to get a build is directly
from this page or from [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/theomnigrid).

Six levels instead of an on/off switch. A Security Shield that blocks
malware, phishing, and fake-shop domains independently of your ad-blocking
level — it's on even when you turn everything else off. Per-site trust, an
element picker, custom filter lists, and a dashboard in five languages.
No telemetry, no accounts, no server — nothing to opt out of, because
there's nothing collecting anything in the first place.

OmniBlock is honest about its limits, too: on Chromium, no Manifest V3
extension can match what MV2-era uBlock Origin could do, because Chrome
removed the API that made it possible. On Firefox, OmniBlock plays in
uBlock Origin's own league. Both of those statements are still true after
you become a patron — this page won't tell you otherwise.

## What supporters get

Every tier gets the packaged, ready-to-install build for both browsers,
the complete corresponding source, and new releases as they land — that's
the whole point of supporting a donationware project instead of a store
listing.

- The complete source, as the GPL requires.
- Release notes explaining what actually changed, not a version bump.

### Tier 2 — Signal · $7/month

### Tier 3 — Vanguard · $15/month

Everything in Signal, plus:

- First look at the bug reports and questions you file. Not a faster
  response promise — just yours read first.
- A direct say on one filter-list or feature decision each quarter,
  discussed rather than voted on.

## What you are not paying for

Exclusivity. OmniBlock is GPL-3.0-or-later: everyone who receives a build
gets the complete source alongside it, and every freedom that license
grants — to use it, study it, modify it, and share it. Nothing in these
tiers changes that, and nothing here is licensed differently to a $15
patron than to someone who asked for a free copy because they couldn't
afford one. What tiers buy is time, priority, and a say in what happens
next — not a better license, and not a version anyone else is locked out
of.
